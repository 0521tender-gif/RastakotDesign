Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 23, 2026

Company: RastakotDesign

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how RastakotDesign ("we", "our", or "us") handles information when you use our application ("the App"), which interacts with the Pinterest API solely for the purpose of publishing pins on behalf of the authenticated user.

We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

2. Data We Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data, images, or other content from you.

  • The App only requests access to your Pinterest account for the sole purpose of creating and publishing pins to your boards.
  • We do not store your Pinterest credentials (password, tokens) on our servers.
  • Any access token obtained through OAuth 2.0 is used only during the active session and is not persisted.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • Publishing pins: The App uses the Pinterest API v5 to create pins with the images, titles, and descriptions you provide.
  • No analytics or tracking: We do not use analytics tools, cookies, or any tracking mechanisms.
  • No third-party sharing: We never share your data with third parties.

4. Data Storage

  • We do not operate any database or storage system that retains user data.
  • All processing is performed in real-time during your use of the App.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Revoke the App's access to your Pinterest account at any time via your Pinterest account settings.
  • Ask us to delete any data we might have (although we store none).
