Privacy Policy

App: Wan Drive Uploader (a personal tool, used only by its developer).

What the app does

The app saves video files that the user generates in their own notebook to the user's own Google Drive. It uses only the drive.file permission, so it can access only the files and folders it created itself.

Data we collect

We do not collect, store, share or sell any personal data. Credentials are kept only in the user's own private Kaggle Secrets and are used solely to upload files to the user's Drive.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions
